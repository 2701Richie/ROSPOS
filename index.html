Encrypted Google Drive backups

Optional. Use your own Google account; sales stay available offline.

Checking cloud backup settings…

Connect your own Google account (one-time setup on the ROSPOS computer)
  1. Install rclone on the ROSPOS computer (Arch: sudo pacman -S rclone).
  2. Open a terminal as the same user that runs ROSPOS and enter rclone config. Create a Google Drive remote, authorize the Google account in your browser, and choose your account.
  3. In rclone config, create a second remote of type crypt whose storage points to the Google Drive remote (for example mydrive:ROSPOS-Backups). Choose a strong crypt password, standard filename encryption, and encrypted directory names. Store its recovery password safely outside this computer.
  4. Return here, refresh accounts, choose the encrypted remote, and enable automatic backups. Google sign-in occurs in rclone, not inside ROSPOS.

Never upload an unencrypted database to Drive. Disconnecting here does not revoke access in Google; to revoke access, also remove its rclone remote or revoke the app from your Google Account.
